Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Shop details: name, address, phone, GSTIN, drug licence.
  • Business records you enter: medicines, bills, customers and their phone numbers, suppliers, purchases and payments.
  • Technical records: sign-in times, IP addresses and an activity log of changes.

Why we use it

Only to run the service for you: to sign you in, store your records, send verification codes, keep the service secure, and contact you about your account. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Customer details you enter

Your customers' names and phone numbers are your records. You decide what to store and you are responsible for having a fair reason to store it. Do not enter more than you need.

Who else handles data

Our hosting provider stores the data. Google sends verification emails and provides Google sign-in if you choose it. When you press a WhatsApp button, your browser opens WhatsApp with the message, and WhatsApp's own policy applies.

Keeping it safe

Connections are encrypted, each shop's data is kept apart, staff logins have limited permissions, and backups are made regularly. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

How long we keep it

While your account is active, and for as long as tax or other laws require. You can ask us to delete your account. Backups are replaced over time.

Your rights

You can view, correct and download your data inside the app, and contact us to delete it or to raise a concern. We will reply within a reasonable time, as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Contact

Use the support details shown on the website or inside the app.
